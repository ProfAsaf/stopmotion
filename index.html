Before you start

  1. Keep the iPad still. Use a stand, or lean it on books. Tape marks around its feet help you put it back after a bump.
  2. Use a lamp and close the curtains, so the light stays the same.
  3. Stick toys down with poster putty so they don't tip over.
  4. Move things a tiny bit between photos. The see-through ghost shows your last photo.
